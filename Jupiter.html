Jupiter
The Beautiful Nightmare

Introduction

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest in the solar system, more than twice as massive as all the other planets combined. It is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium, with no solid surface to stand on. Its banded clouds, powerful storms, and dozens of moons make it a miniature solar system of its own.

Diameter About 139,800 km Distance from the Sun About 778 million km Length of a year About 11.9 Earth years Length of a day About 10 hours Temperature at the cloud tops About -110 °C Moons 95 confirmed (and counting)

Research Data

Missions to Jupiter

  • 1973: NASA's Pioneer 10 becomes the first spacecraft to fly past Jupiter. Pioneer 11 follows in 1974.
  • 1979: Voyager 1 and 2 fly by, discovering Jupiter's faint rings and active volcanoes on the moon Io.
  • 1995: Galileo becomes the first spacecraft to orbit Jupiter and drops a probe into its atmosphere. The mission ends in 2003 when Galileo is deliberately sent into the planet.
  • 2007: New Horizons uses Jupiter's gravity for a speed boost on its way to Pluto.
  • 2016: NASA's Juno arrives in a polar orbit to study Jupiter's interior, magnetic field, and auroras.
  • 2023: ESA launches JUICE to study Jupiter's icy moons, with arrival planned for the early 2030s.
  • 2024: NASA launches Europa Clipper to study Europa's ocean, with arrival planned for around 2030.
