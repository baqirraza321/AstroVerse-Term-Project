    • Venus
    The Evening Star and Earth's "Evil Twin"

    Introduction

    Venus is the second planet from the Sun and Earth’s closest planetary neighbor. Similar in size, mass, and composition to Earth, it is often called Earth’s twin though its crushing pressure, toxic clouds, and extreme heat make it one of the most hostile worlds in the solar system.

    Research Data

    1. A Day Longer Than Its Year

      2. Venus has an exceptionally slow rotational speed, taking 243 Earth days to turn once on its axis. Because it orbits the Sun in 225 Earth days, a single "day" on Venus is actually longer than its "year".

    2. Backward (Retrograde) Rotation

      3. Unlike almost all other planets in the solar system, Venus spins clockwise (east to west) on its axis. On Venus, the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east.

    3. Runaway Greenhouse Effect

      4. Despite being further from the Sun than Mercury, Venus is the hottest planet in our solar system. Its thick, dense atmosphere is composed of 96.5% carbon dioxide with clouds of corrosive sulfuric acid. This creates an extreme greenhouse effect that traps heat, making the surface hot enough to melt lead.

    4. Volcanic & Mountainous Landscape

      5. Radar mapping reveals a surface dominated by volcanic plains, vast lava flows, and thousands of volcanoes. It features complex mountain ranges—such as Maxwell Montes, which towers higher than Mount Everest.
