    Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system and nearest to the Sun. It's only slightly larger than Earth's Moon.
    From the surface of Mercury, the Sun would appear more than three times as large as it does when viewed from Earth, and the sunlight would be as much as seven times brighter.

    Research Data

    Size and Gravity: Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system, featuring a radius of 2,439.7 km and a surface gravity just 38% of Earth's.

    Extreme Orbit: A single year on Mercury takes only 87.97 Earth days, while a single rotation on its axis takes 58.65 Earth days.

    Temperatures: Due to a lack of atmosphere, temperatures fluctuate wildly from -180°C (-290°F) at night to 430°C (800°F) during the day.

    Space Missions: It has been studied by NASA's Mariner 10 and MESSENGER missions, and is currently being mapped by the BepiColombo spacecraft.
