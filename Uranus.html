Uranus
Where it literally rains diamonds.

Introduction

Spins sideways

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun, an ice giant made mostly of water, ammonia, and methane over a small rocky core. Methane in its atmosphere absorbs red light, which gives it a pale blue-green colour. Its most unusual feature is its tilt: Uranus rotates on its side, so it rolls around the Sun like a ball. It was the first planet discovered with a telescope.

Research Data

Exploring Uranus

  • 1781: William Herschel discovers Uranus with a telescope, the first planet found that way.
  • 1977: Astronomers discover Uranus's rings when the planet passes in front of a star and the starlight blinks.
  • 1986: NASA's Voyager 2 makes the only flyby ever, finding 10 new moons and two new rings, and revealing the surprisingly bland, blue-green atmosphere.
  • 2023: The James Webb Space Telescope images Uranus and its rings in sharp infrared detail.
