Earth

Introduction

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only place in the universe we know to have life. About 71% of its surface is covered in liquid water, and a thin atmosphere protects everything living on it. It formed around 4.5 billion years ago.

Research Data

  • 1957: The Soviet Union launches Sputnik 1, the first artificial satellite.
  • 1958: The US launches Explorer 1, whose instruments reveal the Van Allen radiation belts around Earth.
  • 1960: TIROS-1 becomes the first weather satellite, sending back images of Earth's clouds.
  • 1968: Apollo 8 astronauts photograph Earthrise over the Moon.
  • 1972: Apollo 17 captures the Blue Marble photo, and Landsat 1 starts the longest continuous record of Earth's land from space.
  • 1990: Voyager 1 photographs Earth as a tiny speck from about 6 billion km away.
  • 2000: The International Space Station becomes continuously crewed, orbiting about 400 km up.
