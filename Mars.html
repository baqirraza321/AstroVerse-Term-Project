Mars
The destination is ancient. The journey is just beginning.

Introduction

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun, a cold desert world with a rusty red surface coloured by iron oxide. Its thin atmosphere is mostly carbon dioxide, but dried-up riverbeds and lake basins show that liquid water once flowed here. That makes Mars the top target in the search for past life beyond Earth.

Diameter 6,779 km Distance from the Sun About 228 million km Length of a year 687 Earth days Length of a day About 24 hours 37 minutes Average surface temperature About -63 °C Moons 2 (Phobos and Deimos)

Research Data

Missions to Mars

  • 1965: NASA's Mariner 4 makes the first successful flyby of Mars and sends back the first close-up photos.
  • 1971: Mariner 9 becomes the first spacecraft to orbit another planet, and maps most of Mars.
  • 1976: NASA's Viking 1 and 2 landers touch down and study the surface for years.
  • 1997: Mars Pathfinder lands with Sojourner, the first rover on Mars.
  • 2004: The twin rovers Spirit and Opportunity land and find evidence that water once flowed on Mars.
  • 2012: Curiosity lands in Gale Crater to study whether Mars could once have supported microbial life.
  • 2018: The InSight lander lands and records marsquakes, revealing the planet's inner structure.
  • 2021: Perseverance lands in Jezero Crater and begins collecting rock samples. It carries Ingenuity, the first helicopter to fly on another planet.
